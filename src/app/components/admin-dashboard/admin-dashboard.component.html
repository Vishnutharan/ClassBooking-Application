What is "DBS Verified" in the UK?

🛡️ Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) — UK Government Check

In the United Kingdom (England, Wales & Northern Ireland; PVG in Scotland), the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) is the official UK Home Office agency that conducts criminal record and safeguarding checks for anyone working with children or young people under 18.

1. Enhanced DBS Check

The highest level of UK background check required for 1-to-1 tutors teaching GCSE, A-Level, and 11+ school pupils.

2. Children's Barred List

Confirms the educator is cleared against the UK Department for Education (DfE) Children's Barred List.

3. QTS & University ID

Verified Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) or UK university degree credentials alongside photo ID.
